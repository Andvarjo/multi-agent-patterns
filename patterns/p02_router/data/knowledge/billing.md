# Ledgerly — Billing knowledge base

## Plans
- **Free:** $0. Up to 5 invoices per month, 1 user.
- **Pro:** $15/month or $144/year. Unlimited invoices, 1 user, recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders.
- **Team:** $39/month or $372/year. Up to 5 users included; each extra user costs $8/month. Adds roles and invoice approvals.

Subscriptions are charged in USD only.

## Payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Bank transfer is accepted only for the annual Team plan.

## Plan changes
- **Upgrades** take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the current billing cycle.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- When downgrading to Free, team members other than the owner lose access. Their data is kept and access returns if the account upgrades again.

## Refunds
- Annual plans are refundable within 14 days of purchase.
- Monthly plans are not refundable.
- Canceling a subscription or deleting an account does not trigger a prorated refund for the remaining time; only the 14-day window for annual plans applies.

## Failed payments
If a subscription payment fails, Ledgerly retries the charge 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account moves to the Free plan. No data is deleted.

## Taxes
VAT is added for customers in the EU based on the billing address. Businesses can remove VAT by adding a valid VAT ID in Settings > Billing (reverse charge).

## Discounts
Registered nonprofits get 30% off any paid plan. They must request it through support and provide proof of nonprofit status.

## Subscription invoices
Ledgerly's invoices for your subscription are in Settings > Billing > Billing history, downloadable as PDF.

## Ledgerly Payments (fees for collecting money from your clients)
- Card payments: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.
- ACH bank payments: 1% per transaction, capped at $10.
- Payouts reach your bank account in 2 business days.
