# Ledgerly — Account knowledge base

## Sign-in
- Sign up and sign in with email and password, or with Google.
- Password reset links are valid for 1 hour. If a link expired, request a new one from the sign-in page.
- After 10 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes.
- Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity. You can sign out of all devices in Settings > Security.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
- 2FA works with an authenticator app or SMS.
- When enabling 2FA you receive 10 one-time recovery codes.
- If you lose both your 2FA device and your recovery codes, contact support. Identity verification is required and recovery takes up to 3 business days.

## Profile
- To change your sign-in email, go to Settings > Profile. A confirmation is sent to both the old and the new address.
- Company name, logo and tax ID shown on invoices are managed in Settings > Business profile.

## Team members and roles
- The owner or an admin invites people by email from Settings > Team. Invitations expire after 7 days.
- Roles:
  - **Owner:** full access, including billing. Each account has exactly one owner.
  - **Admin:** full access except transferring ownership and deleting the account.
  - **Member:** can create and send invoices but cannot see billing or settings.
- Ownership can be transferred in Settings > Team. The new owner must already be an admin.

## Deleting an account
- Settings > Account > Delete account.
- There is a 30-day grace period during which the account can be restored by signing in. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted.
- Deleting an account cancels any active subscription. Refunds follow the billing policies.
